Zephyr Garden Notes is an independent gardening journal for people who want to understand the why behind the how.

Our Story

Zephyr Garden Notes began in 2021 as a simple email newsletter between two friends — Elara, a soil scientist, and Marcus, a lifelong vegetable grower. They were tired of gardening advice that felt either too shallow or too academic. They wanted something in between: practical, research-backed, and honest about the fact that gardening is messy, seasonal, and sometimes heartbreaking.

Today, Zephyr is read by thousands of gardeners around the world. We remain independent — no sponsored content, no affiliate-driven recommendations, no fluff. Just real dirt, real experiments, and a deep respect for the living soil beneath our feet.

We believe that the best gardens are built slowly, with observation and patience. And we believe that sharing what we learn — including our failures — makes us all better growers.

Meet the Writers

Elara Wren

Editor & Soil Nerd

Elara has a background in soil ecology and a deep obsession with compost. She writes our soil guides, seasonal essays, and anything that involves a microscope. Her garden is a chaotic laboratory of cover crops and native perennials.

Marcus Hale

Growing Guides

Marcus has been growing vegetables for over two decades. He specializes in tomatoes, peppers, and anything you can eat straight off the vine. He writes our practical growing guides and is a firm believer in the "less is more" approach to pruning.

Priya Nair

Container & Small Space

Priya gardens on a windy balcony in the city. She writes about container growing, vertical gardens, and making the most of tiny spaces. Her specialty is dwarf varieties and herbs that thrive in pots.

What We Stand For

  • Soil first. Healthy soil grows healthy plants. We prioritize compost, mulch, and no-till methods over quick fixes.
  • Observation over intervention. The best gardeners spend more time looking than doing. We encourage slow, thoughtful gardening.
  • Honesty about failure. Not every experiment works. We share our mistakes so you can avoid them — or at least laugh along.
  • Independence. No sponsored posts, no hidden ads. Our only loyalty is to our readers and the soil.
  • Accessible science. We read the research so you don't have to. Every guide is grounded in real horticultural science, explained in plain language.

What We're Not

  • A glossy lifestyle magazine. We don't care about perfect photoshoots.
  • A source of quick-fix hacks. Gardening takes time.
  • Affiliated with any chemical or garden product company.
  • Afraid to say "I don't know." When we're unsure, we say so.
  • For people who want a perfectly manicured lawn. We're wilder than that.
