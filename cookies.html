Cookies Policy

Last updated: April 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how Zephyr Garden Notes uses cookies and similar technologies to recognise you when you visit our website. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

Short version: We use a small number of cookies. Some are essential for the site to work. One is for basic, privacy-friendly analytics. We don't use advertising cookies or track you across other sites. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Zephyr Garden Notes) are called "first-party cookies". Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies". Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionality to be provided on or through the website (e.g., analytics).

2. How we use cookies

We use first-party cookies for several reasons. Some cookies are required for technical reasons for our website to operate, and we refer to these as "essential" or "strictly necessary" cookies. Other cookies enable us to track and target the interests of our users to enhance the experience on our website. For example, we use a privacy-focused analytics tool to understand how visitors use the site.

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising, and we do not share cookie data with advertising networks.

3. Types of cookies we use

The specific types of first-party cookies served through our website and the purposes they perform are described below:

Essential website cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our website and to use some of its features, such as accessing secure areas. Because these cookies are strictly necessary to deliver the website, you cannot refuse them without impacting how our site functions. You can block or delete them by changing your browser settings, but this may affect site functionality.

  • Cookie preference: Remembers whether you have accepted our cookie notice.
  • Form state: Helps maintain your input if you fill out a contact form and navigate away temporarily.

Analytics and performance cookies

These cookies are used to collect information about traffic to our site and how users use our site. The information gathered does not identify any individual visitor. It includes the number of visitors to our site, the websites that referred them, the pages they visited, the time of day they visited, and whether they have visited before. We use this information to help operate our site more efficiently, to gather broad demographic information, and to monitor the level of activity on our site.

  • Analytics: We use a lightweight, privacy-friendly analytics tool that anonymises IP addresses. It sets a cookie to distinguish unique visitors and track page views.

Functionality cookies

These cookies are used to enhance the performance and functionality of our site but are non-essential to its use. However, without these cookies, certain functionality may become unavailable.

  • Newsletter signup: If you subscribe, a cookie may remember your subscription status to avoid showing the signup form repeatedly.

4. How can you control cookies?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website though your access to some functionality and areas may be restricted.

How you can manage cookies depends on your browser. Here are links to the support pages for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

To opt out of analytics tracking, you can also use the cookie consent banner (if shown) or simply block third-party cookies in your browser settings.

5. Third-party cookies

We do not allow third-party advertisers to set cookies on our site. The only third-party services we may use are:

  • Email service provider: If you subscribe to our newsletter, our email provider may set a cookie when you click a link in an email. This is used solely for analytics about email performance.
  • Hosting: Our hosting provider may set essential security cookies.

We have no control over these third-party cookies. Please check the relevant third party's privacy policy for more information.

6. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time in order to reflect, for example, changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please re-visit this Cookies Policy regularly to stay informed about our use of cookies and related technologies.

The date at the top of this Cookies Policy indicates when it was last updated.

7. More information

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or other technologies, please email us at privacy@zephyrgardennotes.com or use our contact form.

For more general information about cookies and how to manage them, you may find allaboutcookies.org useful.
