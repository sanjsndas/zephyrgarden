Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need to test my soil?

A basic pH test and a look at texture can save you years of frustration. Most garden centers offer affordable tests, or you can send a sample to a lab.

What's the best time to water?

Early morning is ideal — it reduces evaporation and lets foliage dry before evening, which helps prevent fungal diseases.

How do I deal with slugs without chemicals?

Beer traps, copper tape, diatomaceous earth, and evening hand-picking are all effective. Encourage birds and hedgehogs too.

Is raised bed gardening better than in-ground?

It depends. Raised beds offer better drainage and control, but in-ground gardening builds long-term soil health and requires less watering once established.
