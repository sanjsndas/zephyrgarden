Quick answers before you write

Can you identify a plant or disease for me?

We'll try if you include clear photos and your growing zone, but we're not a diagnostic lab. For fast, accurate ID, try a local extension office or a plant identification app.

Do you accept sponsored posts or advertising?

No. Zephyr Garden Notes is fully independent and ad-free. We don't publish sponsored content or accept payment for coverage.

I found a typo or factual error. What should I do?

Please tell us! Use the form above and choose "Correction or update." We appreciate accuracy and will fix errors promptly.

Can I republish your articles?

We're flattered, but we ask that you don't republish full articles without permission. Short quotes with a link back are always welcome.
