Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

At Zephyr Garden Notes, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have. We keep things simple — no jargon, no hidden data sales.

Short version: We collect only what's needed to run the blog and reply to you. We don't sell your data. We don't run ads. We use a few functional cookies and one analytics tool to understand what readers find useful.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in two ways: what you give us directly, and what's collected automatically when you visit.

Information you provide

  • Contact form: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message so we can reply.
  • Newsletter: If you subscribe, we collect your email address to send you weekly notes. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Comments: If we enable comments on an article, your name, email, and comment content are stored. (We currently do not have comments enabled.)

Information collected automatically

  • Log data: Like most websites, our server logs your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time of visit. This helps us understand traffic and fix issues.
  • Cookies: We use small text files to remember your preferences (like whether you've dismissed a notice) and to measure site usage. See our Cookies Policy for details.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect only for these purposes:

  • To reply to your messages and questions.
  • To send you the newsletter if you've opted in.
  • To improve our articles, navigation, and site performance.
  • To detect and prevent abuse or spam.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We don't run targeted ads and we don't share your data with advertisers.

3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you're in the European Economic Area, our legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Consent: for the newsletter and non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interest: for responding to contact form messages and maintaining site security.
  • Legal obligation: if we're required to retain records by law.

4. Cookies and tracking

We use a minimal set of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: needed for the site to function (e.g., remembering form submissions). These can't be turned off.
  • Analytics cookies: we use a privacy-friendly analytics tool (self-hosted or with IP anonymization) to count visits and see which articles are popular. No personal profiles are built.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. See our full Cookies Policy.

5. Third-party services

We keep third-party services to a minimum. Currently:

  • Email delivery: We use a reputable newsletter service (e.g., Buttondown or Mailcoach) to send emails. They only process your email address on our behalf.
  • Hosting: Our site is hosted on a secure server. Standard server logs are kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any ad networks. If this changes, we'll update this policy and notify subscribers.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Contact form messages: Kept for up to 12 months so we can reference past conversations, then deleted.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Kept until you unsubscribe, plus a short period for suppression (so we don't accidentally email you again).
  • Server logs: Retained for 30 days, then automatically deleted.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@zephyrgardennotes.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

Zephyr Garden Notes is not directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll post a notice on the homepage.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out:

  • Email: privacy@zephyrgardennotes.com
  • Or use our contact form.

We're a small team, but we take privacy seriously and will do our best to help.
